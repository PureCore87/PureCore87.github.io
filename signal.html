Signal

Understand your connection.

Closed beta

Know the network around you.

A strong signal is only part of the picture. Signal brings together reception data, response times and practical diagnostic tools to help you understand what your connection is doing.

Start with the connection

Reception. Radio conditions. Context.

01 / Wi-Fi

See your wireless connection.

Inspect signal strength, SSID, band, channel, frequency and Android-reported link rates. Choose automatic card selection or show Wi-Fi and mobile information together.

02 / Mobile

Look beyond the signal bars.

Explore available 4G and 5G measurements, including RSRP, RSRQ, RSSI and SINR. A radio-quality indicator adds an assessment based on available current measurements.

Available information depends on Android, your hardware, permissions and the connection. A Wi-Fi link rate describes the negotiated radio link; it is not measured internet throughput.

Focused diagnostic tools

Ask a more specific question.

03 / Network Check

Is the connection responsive?

Measure latency, jitter and available packet loss, with a combined quality assessment and a readable diagnosis. When ICMP results are insufficient, the app can use a separately identified HTTPS response-time fallback.

04 / Target Check

How does this target respond?

Investigate a domain, IP address or URL. Explore DNS, IPv4 and IPv6 ping results, TCP connections, TLS and HTTP/HTTPS responses, including available timing and status information.

05 / Local discovery

Which devices answer?

Find responding devices in the scanned local Wi-Fi IPv4 range. View available names, gateway information and open tested TCP ports, then send a selected address directly to Target Check.

06 / Channel analysis

Compare your Wi-Fi environment.

Explore visible networks across supported 2.4, 5 and 6 GHz bands. Collect fresh scan rounds in different areas to build a broader picture of channel conditions.

07 / Repeater placement

Explore suitable positions.

Walk through possible locations while monitoring your phone’s router signal, RX/TX link rates and signal statistics. Stability and fluctuations help put the current position into context.

08 / Language

Use the app in your language.

Choose German, English, Spanish, French, Italian, Turkish, Russian or Portuguese inside the app, or follow your system language.

Local evaluation / Active tests

Diagnosis without an account.

The documented build has no advertising or behavioural analytics integration. Wi-Fi and mobile information is evaluated on your device; Signal is not designed to record a GPS movement history.

Active checks contact their test destinations, DNS services or local devices. Those connections can expose ordinary technical information such as your public IP address. Local evaluation does not mean that active tests work offline.

A little more clarity

Questions about Signal.

Does Signal measure download or upload speed?

The current feature set does not contain a download/upload speed test. Signal examines reception, responsiveness, stability and reachability. A displayed Wi-Fi link rate is not measured internet speed.

What do latency, jitter and packet loss tell me?

ICMP latency describes a test packet’s round trip. Jitter describes variation between response times using the app’s calculation method. Packet loss is the share of sent ICMP test packets without a received reply. An HTTPS fallback is a different response-time measurement and does not manufacture a packet-loss result.

What does the mobile radio-quality indicator mean?

It assesses available RSRP, RSRQ and SINR measurements. It does not measure call audio quality or confirm VoLTE/VoWiFi. Red can also indicate missing, invalid or outdated input, rather than a confirmed poor radio connection.

Will the local scanner find every device?

It shows devices that respond within its bounded search range. Firewalls, VPN rules, client isolation, sleeping devices and closed discovery ports can limit detection. The scanner is not a complete network inventory.

Can it tell whether another device uses Ethernet or Wi-Fi?

An IP address, ping response and port list do not reliably reveal another device’s physical connection type. Signal does not promise a complete Ethernet/Wi-Fi topology.

Does a missing ping response mean a service is offline?

No. A device can block or deprioritise ICMP and still answer HTTPS or other services. Target Check separates the different protocol checks so you can interpret them together.

Does the app guarantee the best repeater position?

No. The tool evaluates your phone’s current Wi-Fi connection and its stability. Building materials, repeater hardware and the final network configuration also affect the result.

Does Signal work with a VPN?

The app includes VPN-related connection and routing handling. Reachability and reported connection details still depend on the VPN configuration and Android policies; compatibility with every setup is not guaranteed.
