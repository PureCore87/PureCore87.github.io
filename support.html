What can I check with Signal?

Signal brings together Wi-Fi and mobile information, network checks, speed testing, channel analysis, a live repeater placement monitor and target checks.

Why are some connection details unavailable?

Android restricts access to certain Wi-Fi and mobile details. Available values depend on your device, Android version, permissions and current connection.

Can a network test use mobile data?

Yes. Tests that send or receive data use the active internet connection. Speed tests in particular can transfer a substantial amount of data. Review the app's mobile data settings before testing.

Why does a target not answer a ping?

A device or service may block or deprioritize ping traffic. A missing ping response alone does not establish that a website or service is unavailable.

Is ARX Core available yet?

ARX Core is currently in development. Further information will appear on its project page.
