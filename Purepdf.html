Paper / Images / Documents

A practical starting point.

01 / Scan & images

Bring paper onto your phone.

Capture paper documents or import existing images such as JPEG and PNG. Add pages, review thumbnails, change the order, rotate or remove a page before export.

02 / PDF import

Edit pages as images.

Import multi-page PDFs for image-based editing. Pages are rendered into images: selectable text, vector elements, interactive forms and digital signatures are not preserved as those original layers in the new PDF.

03 / Text & Office

A simple route from text to PDF.

Import TXT, DOCX, ODT, RTF and HTML. Text is laid out on new A4 pages; DOCX and ODT also include supported table rows and embedded images. RTF and HTML import text content.

04 / Layout expectations

Know what carries across.

Office import is simplified, rather than a full Word-layout conversion. Complex formatting can change or be omitted. For visual fidelity, export a PDF from the original application first. DOC, Excel and PowerPoint import are not included.

Geometry / Preview / Control

Give the page
a clearer shape.

05 / Paper boundaries

Four corners. Your confirmation.

Use suggested document boundaries or set them manually. Check the four paper corners with a magnified detail view, crosshair and arrow controls before accepting the crop.

06 / Horizontal alignment

Straighten a tilted page.

Start with an automatic angle suggestion or adjust the angle manually from −45° to +45°. Fine adjustments and a preview help you judge the result.

07 / Vertical perspective

Correct converging edges.

A separate workflow lets you select edges, adjust vertical perspective and preview the correction. Horizontal tilt and vertical perspective stay independently controllable.

08 / Fine guidance

Keep the details in view.

Adjust guide colours, boundary and dashed-line thickness, corner size and the pixel-coordinate display. Choose the whole image when no crop is needed.

Automatic detection assists your judgement. Check the result, especially with shadows, weak contrast or obscured paper edges. Deliberate blank space and uneven document margins should be kept when you confirm the boundaries.

Selected information / Personal touches

Redact with precision.
Place your signature.

09 / Precise selection

Small areas, carefully marked.

Mark multiple redaction rectangles. Zoom up to 6×, pan the page and move a whole rectangle, edge or corner with arrow controls in steps of one or five original-image pixels.

10 / Permanent redaction

Replace the selected pixels.

Pending areas remain translucent while editing; the preview shows solid black. Applying a redaction replaces the selected image pixels with black, rather than adding a movable overlay. Applied redactions cannot be undone.

11 / Drawn signature

A signature in your own hand.

Draw with a finger or stylus, use the eraser or reset, and choose black, blue, dark blue, red or green. Position and resize the signature before embedding it into the page image.

12 / Clear distinction

A drawing, without a certificate.

The inserted signature has no verified identity or cryptographic PDF certificate. It is not a qualified electronic signature. Check the recipient’s requirements for your particular document or process.

Redaction does not change external originals, older exports or copies already shared. Keep those originals separately and check that you are sharing the newly redacted version. This feature does not securely erase every copy on the device.

Format / Resolution / Quality

Export for
the task ahead.

13 / Resolution

From 72 to 600 DPI.

Choose 72, 150, 200, 300, 400 or 600 DPI. More DPI can retain detail when the source supports it; enlarging a low-resolution image does not create missing information.

14 / Paper & orientation

Choose how the page fits.

Use image format, A3, A4, A5, A6, Letter or Legal, with automatic, portrait or landscape orientation. Set an additional margin of 0, 5, 10, 15 or 20 mm.

15 / Image quality

Balance detail and file size.

Original avoids additional JPEG losses during PDF embedding. High, Medium and Compact use JPEG quality settings of 90, 75 and 60. Earlier corrections, scaling or source-image losses are not reversed.

16 / Optional optimisation

A more even-looking page.

A single export option combines white balance, illumination adjustment, gentle noise reduction and limited sharpening. It can reduce uneven lighting; strong shadows, glare or blur can remain.

What does a zero margin do?

At 0 mm, the PDF page adapts to the image’s aspect ratio without cropping or distorting its content. Its physical dimensions can differ from the selected standard paper format. White areas already inside the image remain. Borderless printing also depends on the printer.

Image optimisation is applied during PDF export, rather than to the normal editing view. Export processes image regions in stages to reduce memory use; page count, source size and device capability still matter.

Your document workflow.

PurePDF’s own editing and export functions process documents on the Android device without a required PurePDF account. Choose your input files and save destination through Android. A cloud document provider or another app you share with has its own behaviour.

Questions about PurePDF.

Can I combine several pages into one PDF?

Yes. Add pages, review their thumbnails, change the order and export the selected document as one multi-page PDF.

Does PurePDF create searchable PDFs?

The documented version creates image-based PDFs. An OCR text layer for full-text search and text selection is not included.

Will an imported PDF keep its digital signature?

No. Import renders the pages into images. The newly created PDF does not preserve the original digital-signature, form, vector or selectable-text layers. Keep digitally signed original files unchanged.

Is a drawn signature a certified digital signature?

No. It is a drawing embedded into the page image, without verified identity or a cryptographic certificate. Acceptance depends on the recipient and the requirements of the particular process.

Does redaction also remove my original file?

No. Applying a redaction replaces the selected pixels in the edited page. External originals, older exports and previously shared copies remain unchanged. Check the final PDF before sharing it.

Will Word documents look exactly like the original?

DOCX and ODT are laid out again in a simplified form. Complex formatting is not fully reproduced. Export to PDF in the original application first when visual fidelity matters.

Can it fix every shadow or blurred photo?

No. Optional export optimisation can improve uneven illumination, but cannot reliably repair strong glare, severe shadows, overexposure or missing detail. A well-lit, sharp capture is the best starting point.
