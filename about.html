The meaning behind PureCore.

Pure stands for clarity and a focus on what matters. Core is the essential part: the heart of an idea, a tool or an experience.

Together, PureCore describes an approach to software: begin with a practical purpose, explain things clearly and pay attention to the details that make a tool useful.

Different tools. The same care.

Signal helps you understand Wi-Fi, mobile and network behaviour. ARX Core brings credentials and personal files together in a local encrypted vault. Their jobs are different; both are built around focused workflows and understandable information.

Honest about the details.

A reported link rate is not an internet speed measurement. A verified backup is not a promise that every device or failure scenario has been tested. Product pages explain what the apps do, where their limits are and which stage of development they have reached.
